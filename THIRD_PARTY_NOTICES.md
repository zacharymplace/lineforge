## Stockfish.js

LineForge includes the local browser engine package `stockfish` for optional, user-triggered position analysis.

Stockfish.js is a WebAssembly build of the Stockfish chess engine. The package includes its license text in `node_modules/stockfish/Copying.txt` after installation.
